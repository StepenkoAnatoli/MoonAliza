## Build intent

The Windows desktop AI coding assistant developed in this repository (an Electron app, repository Moonzila, earlier product name MoonAliza) needs a product name. Selenic was refused by the sibling project `docs/research/2026-10-12-selenic-name-check` (a live US registration, PARASOFT SELENIC, Class 9, and a current AI developer tool sold as Selenic). The sibling screen `docs/research/2026-10-12-product-name-screen` ranked **Selunara** first of five candidates, flagged only for a registered .com. This project is the full check of Selunara. Nothing is built from it: it is a naming decision. Done means a reader who has never seen the project can tell, from pages captured on 2026-10-12: (a) whether Justia's index of USPTO filings lists a US mark for SELUNARA or for the close variants SELUNA, SELUNAR, SELENARA and SALUNARA, and for any hit its owner, goods, classes and status from the USPTO's own TSDR record; (b) whether any software or AI product or company trades as Selunara (two pages of one web search, and the site of any product it shows); (c) whether the names `selunara` on GitHub (repositories and the account), npm (the package and the @selunara scope) and PyPI are taken; (d) whether selunara.com, .dev, .app, .ai and .io are registered, and what a registered one serves; (e) whether the Microsoft Store's own search lists an app named Selunara; (f) whether any dictionary gives the word a meaning; and (g) for every register this transport cannot read (the USPTO's own search, TMview/EUIPO, the WIPO Global Brand Database), the exact manual search to run. The verdict - free, caution or conflict, on the scale below - is a risk call from public records, not legal advice and not a clearance.
