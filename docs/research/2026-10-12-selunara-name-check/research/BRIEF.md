## Decision

**Verdict: CAUTION, and a stronger caution than this brief first gave.** Selunara is not free. The exact name is already registered in the US by someone else, for coaching services. A close variant is pending for medical software. No conflict on the scale was found in the sources read.

**What this means for the operator.** Two live US marks sit beside the name: SÉLUNARA, one accent away, registered to an individual for coaching and personal-development services in Class 041, and SELUNA, Selunara's first six letters, Seluna Ltd's pending application for medical software in Classes 9 and 42. This is a risk call from public records captured on 2026-10-12, on the scale written before collection (research/DISCOVERY.md, Already decided). It is not legal advice and not a clearance.
