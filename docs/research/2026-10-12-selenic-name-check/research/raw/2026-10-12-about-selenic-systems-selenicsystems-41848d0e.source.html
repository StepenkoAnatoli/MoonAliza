About

About Selenic Systems

We're an independent software company building a carefully-made collection of mobile apps — and looking after them for the long term.

What we do

Selenic Systems designs and builds mobile applications. Rather than chasing a long roadmap, we keep our range deliberately focused so we can give each app the attention it deserves — thoughtful design, dependable behaviour, and steady maintenance over time.

You can see everything we currently make on our software page. Each app has its own home, with support and, where it helps, full documentation.

How we build

We care about craft over scale. That means sweating the details most people never notice, choosing reliability over novelty, and shipping software that respects the people who use it — no clutter, no dark patterns, no needless data collection.

When something has our name on it, we want it to feel considered from the first tap to the last. That standard is easier to hold when you focus on doing things well rather than doing everything quickly.

Software in orbit

Our tagline, Software in orbit, is how we think about the work: apps that quietly keep doing their job, dependable and in their proper place. Present when you need them, unobtrusive when you don't.

Where we are
