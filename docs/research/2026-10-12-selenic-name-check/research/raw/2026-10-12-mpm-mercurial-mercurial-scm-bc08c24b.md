# Olivia Mackall

Olivia Mackall was the primary Mercurial author and former project leader. She has been involved in open source development since the early 1990s and started developing Mercurial in April 2005; she [retired](https://wiki.mercurial-scm.org/mpm/transition) from the project in 2016.
