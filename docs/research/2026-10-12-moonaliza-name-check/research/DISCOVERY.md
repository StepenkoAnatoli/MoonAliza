## Build intent

The Windows desktop AI coding assistant developed in this repository (an Electron app; repository github.com/StepenkoAnatoli/MoonAliza, development name Moonzila) needs a product name. Its original name is **MoonAliza**, and the app's installed identifiers already use it (electron-builder.yml: `appId: com.moonaliza.desktop`, native helper `MoonAlizaHost.exe`). The sibling projects under `docs/research/` checked replacements: Selenic was refused (`2026-10-12-selenic-name-check`), and Selunara, ranked first by the screen (`2026-10-12-product-name-screen`), turned out to be a registered US mark, SÉLUNARA, in Class 041 (`2026-10-12-selunara-name-check`, its E-21). This project checks whether keeping MoonAliza is free. Nothing is built from it: it is a naming decision. Done means a reader who has never seen the project can tell, from pages captured on 2026-10-12: (a) whether Justia's index of USPTO filings lists a US mark for MOONALIZA, for the two-word MOON ALIZA, or for the close variants MOONALISA and MONALIZA, with owner and goods for any hit; (b) whether that index lists MONA LISA filings in International Class 9 or 42 (software), and which; (c) whether any software or AI product or company trades as MoonAliza on page 1 of one web search; (d) whether GitHub has repositories named moonaliza and whether the account github.com/moonaliza exists; (e) whether moonaliza.com is registered, since when and through whom; and (f) for every record this budget or this transport cannot reach - the TSDR status of any hit, the USPTO's own search, EU and international registers, other domains, company registers, other namespaces - the exact day-one step. The verdict - free so far, caution or conflict, on the scale under Already decided - is a risk call from public records, not legal advice and not a clearance. An absence in a secondary index is not an absence on the register: the Selunara check closed on Justia's empty result and the USPTO's own search then found the accented SÉLUNARA (its U-01), so every absence here is worded as what the captured page shows and no more.
