## Coverage notes (per dimension)

- D-1 (access): Justia's search, Google's results page, GitHub's REST search and the Verisign RDAP server are public pages read without an account, and each rendered for this transport in the sibling projects. The USPTO's own search, TMview and the WIPO Global Brand Database are JavaScript applications this transport could not drive there (U-05, U-06); an RDAP 404 for an unregistered domain is an error page the kit does not keep (U-11); later search pages failed or misled in the Selunara check (U-15).
- D-4 (legality of the intended use): the use is a product name, so legality is trademark conflict and confusion with a current product; S-1 and S-2 carry the marks (U-01 to U-06) and S-3 the uses in trade (U-07).
- D-6 (freshness): every capture is dated 2026-10-12; filings, domain holdings and products change, and the brief says what to re-check before the name is adopted.
- D-8 (platform): the product is a Windows desktop app, so the Microsoft Store is the platform check; it is outside this budget and is a day-one step (U-14).
- D-9 (obtainability): what this budget or this transport cannot reach is a KNOWN-UNKNOWN with its manual step (U-02, U-05, U-06, U-11, U-12, U-13, U-14, U-15, U-16). The 10 credits bought every page the order required and nothing more: no TSDR record for a Justia hit (U-16) and no page past the first of any list (U-02, U-15, U-16).
- D-2, D-3, D-5, D-7: dismissed with the reasons in the table.
